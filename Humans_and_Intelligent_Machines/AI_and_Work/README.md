# AI and the future of work

[Read the essay](essay.pdf) — a five-page Humans and Intelligent Machines coursework essay on the ethical response to AI's effects on human work.

The essay compares resistance to AI, guided adoption and an ambition to eliminate work. It argues for guided adoption, drawing on economic evidence, Rawlsian reasoning, consequentialism and considerations of virtue, and discusses responsibilities for employers, workers and governments.

This is a completed argumentative essay with a conclusion and bibliography, rather than a forecasting model or an empirical study. It demonstrates synthesis across technical change, economics and ethics. The cited evidence and policy context reflect the assignment period in 2024; the essay is not presented as a current assessment of employment impacts. Its analogy between immigration and AI is part of the author's argument, not causal evidence that their effects are equivalent.
