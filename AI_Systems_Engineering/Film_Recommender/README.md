# Film recommender system design

[Read the design report](design_report.pdf) — an eight-page AI Systems Engineering coursework proposal for a film recommendation service with a conversational voice assistant.

The report connects user, application, AI and data layers; distinguishes consumer, advertising and research clients; sets out functional and non-functional requirements; and discusses recommendation quality, bias, privacy, deployment stages and telemetry.

This is a completed design assignment. It demonstrates architectural reasoning and the connection between product goals and operational measures. It does not include an implemented service, trained recommender, measured user outcomes or a deployment.

The PDF is the original report, preserved without content changes. Its cloud progression and business assumptions are proposals, not validated cost or security conclusions. Production work would require concrete service contracts, measurable service objectives, cost modelling and detailed privacy and security design. The report's regulatory references are part of the historical coursework and are not a current compliance assessment.
